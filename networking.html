Participation Prerequisites

To ensure fair pairing, safety, and smooth cross-border communication, all participants must meet two core requirements:

✓ Verified Mensa Member

You must be a current member in good standing of a recognized National Mensa or a Direct International Member.

✓ Working English Proficiency

As our matching pool spans over 90 countries, conversations are conducted in English unless you and your match mutually decide otherwise.

How It Works

A light, automated framework designed to foster meaningful serendipity without unnecessary administrative overhead.

🎯

Two Matches Per Month

Rounds run continuously (1st to 15th, and 16th to the end of the month). Our non-repeating pairing algorithm pairs you with a new participant from around the globe each cycle.

☕

Unhurried Video Calls

Reach out directly to your match via email or messaging to choose a convenient time and platform (Google Meet, Zoom, WhatsApp). Reserve at least 50 minutes so neither party feels rushed.

🌐

Genuine Serendipity

Conversations are organic. Talk about shared intellectual passions, local culture, research projects, professional ventures, or simply enjoy a thoughtful exchange.

Community Guidelines

Mutual courtesy, tolerance, and respect are expected in all meetings and communications.

  • Punctuality & Courtesy: Honor scheduled sessions. With varying international time zones, please give prompt notice if an unexpected schedule conflict arises.
  • Code of Conduct: In alignment with Mensa community standards, all calls must maintain an environment free of harassment. Partisan political campaigns, religious proselytizing, and discriminatory remarks are strictly prohibited.
  • Safety & Compliance: Any participant reported for inappropriate or disrespectful conduct will be warned privately. In cases of recurrence, coordinators reserve the right to suspend or expel the participant from the matching pool.
