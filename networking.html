How It Works

  • Two rounds per month: Runs continuously (1st–15th and 16th–end of month).
  • Automated matching: An algorithm pairs you with a new international peer each round.
  • Direct scheduling: Coordinate directly via email to choose a platform (Meet, Zoom, WhatsApp) for a 60 min call.

Requirements

  • Verified Membership: Current member in good standing of a National Mensa or Direct International Member (18+).
  • Working English: Shared communication language across participants from 90+ countries.
